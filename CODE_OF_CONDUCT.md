# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim to make collaboration respectful, clear, and constructive. Participants must not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized abuse, or deliberate disruption.

## Expected behavior

- communicate in good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas and files, not people;
- respect different languages, backgrounds, and levels of experience;
- keep reports and reviews specific, evidence-based, and actionable;
- protect private data and unpublished project material.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or close contributions that violate this policy. Report concerns privately through the repository maintainer's configured channel.
